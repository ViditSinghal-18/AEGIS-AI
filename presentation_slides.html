Walks through a hospital with a generic handbook of product recalls and drops a 400-page binder of 5,000 alerts on the doctor's desk:

Technical Architecture

The 4-Stage Automated Intelligence Pipeline

End-to-end integration connecting active network reconnaissance with real-time threat intelligence.

STAGE 1
Perimeter Discovery
discovery.py
TCP SYN probes & HTTP service fingerprinting on ports 80, 443, 3000 to identify listening sockets.
STAGE 2
Vulnerability Correlator
correlate.py
Queries Google OSV & NIST NVD for running dependencies, normalizing GHSA and CVE aliases.
STAGE 3
Explainable RBVM Engine
prioritize.py
Blends FIRST.org EPSS exploit ratings, AST reachability, and CISA KEV directives into a unified score.
STAGE 4
Universal Web Auditor
scanner_online.py
External perimeter evaluation of live websites (TLS 1.3, HSTS, CSP, WAF detection) with A+ to F grades.
Mathematical Explainability

The Explainable RBVM Risk Formulation

Replacing rigid CVSS scores with a multi-dimensional, transparent formula.

Score = (0.35 × Base CVSS) + (0.30 × AST Reachability) + (0.25 × FIRST.org EPSS) + (0.10 × Asset Criticality)
Base CVSS (35%)
Theoretical Impact
Industry-standard vulnerability severity metric.
Reachability (30%)
Network & AST Path
Proves whether public ingress packets can reach the execution sink.
FIRST.org EPSS (25%)
Real-World Exploitability
Daily probability that hackers are weaponizing this bug in the wild.
Criticality (10%)
Business Context
Customer data store vs. internal sandbox testing node.
